Bank statement categorization is one of the most time-consuming tasks in bookkeeping. AI-powered categorization promises to eliminate hours of manual work — but how does it actually work under the hood?

## The problem with manual categorization

Every month, bookkeepers download bank feeds, open hundreds of transactions, and assign each to the correct GL account — meals, utilities, travel, payroll, and dozens more. Rules-based systems help for recurring vendors, but edge cases pile up fast.

## How AI categorization works

Modern AI categorization systems typically combine:

- **Pattern matching** on vendor names, amounts, and transaction descriptions
- **Historical learning** from your past categorization decisions
- **Confidence scoring** so low-confidence items route to human review
- **Context from integrations** — linking to invoices, bills, and receipts in your accounting system

## What finance leaders should expect

AI won't replace your accountant, but it can handle 70–90% of routine transactions automatically. The key is integration: categorization works best when connected to QuickBooks, Xero, or your ERP so approved categories post directly without CSV exports.
